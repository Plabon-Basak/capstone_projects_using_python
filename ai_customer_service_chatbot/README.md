# Day 99: AI-Powered Chatbot for Customer Service

A dependency-free CLI support chatbot that performs keyword-based intent scoring, retrieves grounded answers, reports confidence, and escalates unknown requests into tickets.

## Structure
- `app.py` — conversation demo
- `knowledge_base.py` — support intents and approved answers
- `chatbot.py` — intent scoring, response selection, and escalation
- `tickets.py` — support-ticket creation
